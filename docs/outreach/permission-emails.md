# Permission emails (drafts)

Drafts asking listing owners to let the app show their listings. Nothing has been sent.
Replace the `[brackets]`, send them from your own address, and keep each written reply:
the build only imports a partner after you note their acceptance in `data/sources.toml`
(`terms_accepted = "date, by email from …"`).

Three versions, by who you're writing to:

- **A.** Developers of approved PDS, RES and IRS projects (the 188 already in the app).
- **B.** Estate agencies.
- **C.** Property portals and classified sites (for a crawl permission, not a feed).

Where to find addresses: the developer's own website, which the app links to on each
project. Use the contact page, sales or info address. Don't use the contact persons'
emails printed in the EDB PDFs: the app deliberately doesn't reuse that personal data,
and a cold email to a named individual from that list would undercut that.

Before sending, read `docs/legal/partner-terms.md` once more. It is marked a draft for
legal review: the emails promise only what it says.

## A. Developers of approved projects

**Subject:** [Project name] is already in our free Mauritius property app. Add your prices?

Dear [Developer] team,

I'm building a free app that helps people find property in Mauritius in one place,
on a map, in English and French. It works on Android and as a web app on iPhone.

[Project name] in [place] already appears in it, because it's on the Economic
Development Board's public list of approved [PDS / RES / IRS] projects. Today we only
show the official facts (what is being built, where, the scheme, its status) with a
link to your website. That means buyers who find you can't see any prices or what's
still available.

If you'd like, we can show your available units with prices, sizes and photos. It's free:

- **How:** a spreadsheet you email us, or a link to a simple feed if you have one.
  We update it every day, and units you remove disappear from the app the next day.
- **What buyers see:** your listing marked "Foreigners can buy", the price in rupees,
  euros or dollars, and an estimate of the monthly cost. Every listing links to your page.
- **Enquiries:** they go straight to you. We don't sit in between and don't charge
  commission.
- **Control:** you can ask us to remove anything at any time.

The short terms are attached ([partner-terms.pdf]). If that works for you, a reply
saying you accept them, plus your spreadsheet or feed link, is all we need.

If you'd rather we kept showing only the EDB facts, or removed [Project name]
altogether, just say so and we will.

Kind regards,
[Your name]
[App name] · [web app link: https://jeyveen7.github.io/mauritius-property-finder/]

## B. Estate agencies

**Subject:** Free listings for [Agency] in a new Mauritius property app

Dear [Agency] team,

Buyers in Mauritius, especially foreigners, currently search many sites and Facebook
groups to find a plot or an apartment. I'm building a free app that puts everything on
one map, with honest filters: which properties foreigners can buy, plot size in perches
or arpents, price drops, and the same property shown once when several agencies list it.

I'd like to include your listings, with your permission:

- **Free.** No fee and no commission.
- **Easy.** Send us a spreadsheet (we can send the column list) or a feed link if your
  website has one. We update daily, and sold listings drop off automatically.
- **Your brand and your leads.** Every listing names [Agency] and links to your own page,
  so enquiries come to you. If another agency lists the same property, both are shown and linked.
- **Your photos only if you want.** We show photos only if you confirm you own them or
  may share them.
- **Revocable.** Ask us to remove a listing, a photo or everything at any time.

The terms are one page ([partner-terms.pdf], attached). Reply that you accept them and
send the spreadsheet or link, and you'll be live in the next daily update.

Happy to show you the app first: [web app link].

Kind regards,
[Your name]
[Phone, optional]

## C. Portals and classified sites

**Subject:** Permission to link to [Site] listings from a free property map

Dear [Site] team,

I'm building a free, non-commercial [or: "free to use"] app that shows Mauritius
property for sale on one map. I've read your terms of use and understand that reusing
your listings needs your written consent, so I'm asking before doing anything.

What I'd like to do:

- Read your public listing pages at most once a day, with a clearly named crawler that
  respects your robots.txt.
- Show only basic facts (type, place, price, size), with your name on every listing
  and a prominent link sending the buyer to the full advert on [Site].
- Not copy your photos, descriptions or the advertisers' contact details.
- Stop and remove everything within a day if you ask.

This would send you buyers rather than compete for them: the app has no contact form of
its own, and every listing points back to you. If you'd rather provide a feed, or limit
which listings or fields we use, I'm happy to work to your conditions.

Could you confirm by email whether this is acceptable, and under what conditions?

Kind regards,
[Your name]
[App name] · [web app link]
