**Who we are.** [Your name or company], [address], Mauritius, is the controller of the personal data described here under the Data Protection Act 2017. Contact: [email].

**What we collect, and why**

| Data | Why | Legal basis | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|
| A random device identifier and token | So the app remembers your saved items | Our legitimate interest in providing the service you asked for | Until you delete the app or ask us |
| Saved searches and saved properties | To show them to you and alert you | Contract (providing the service) | Until you delete them |
| Push notification token | To send the alerts you switched on | Consent (you can turn notifications off) | Until it expires or you opt out |
| Google account ID and email, or phone number, when you verify | To keep spam and scams off the map before you post | Legitimate interest in preventing fraud | While your account is active |
| Text and photos of posts you add | To publish the listing you asked us to add | Consent | Raw text: deleted after 30 days. Published facts and photos: until removed. |
| Enquiries you send through a listing (which listing, which source, when) | To pass your interest to the advertiser | Contract | 12 months |
| Reports you make | To check listings | Legitimate interest | 12 months |

**What we do not do.** We do not sell your data. We do not copy names or phone numbers from posts you share. Photos you upload are re-encoded, which removes location data your camera stored in them.

**Who else processes it.** Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications; Google sign-in if you choose it). [Your hosting provider]. Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, which receives your IP address when the map loads.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by writing to [email]. You can complain to the Data Protection Office, Mauritius (https://dataprotection.govmu.org).
