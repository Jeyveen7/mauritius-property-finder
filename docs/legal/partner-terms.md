# Partner terms (draft)

*Draft for review. Agencies, developers and sellers accept these before they get a feed key or before we upload their spreadsheet.*

1. **What you send.** You send us property listings you are entitled to advertise: facts (type, location, price, size, scheme), a link to your own page, and, if you choose, photos.
2. **Licence.** You allow us, free of charge, to show those listings and photos in the app and web app, in search results and alerts, and to resize the photos. We may stop showing them at any time. The licence ends for each listing when you remove it from your feed.
3. **Accuracy.** You keep listings accurate and remove them when sold. Listings missing from two feeds in a row are taken down automatically.
4. **Photos.** You only send photos you own or are licensed to share. Tell us which photos to remove and we will remove them promptly.
5. **Leads.** Enquiries go to the page or contact you give us. We record which listing and source each enquiry came from, and can share those counts with you.
6. **Duplicates.** When the same property is listed by several sources, we show it once, with every source listed and linked.
7. **Personal data.** Don't send personal data about sellers or other individuals beyond a business contact. Each of us handles personal data under the Data Protection Act 2017.
8. **Your key.** Keep your feed key secret. We may revoke it if it is misused.
9. **Cost.** There is no charge for listing.
10. **Changes.** We will tell you about changes to these terms by email, 30 days in advance.
